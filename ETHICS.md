# Ethics and Testing Boundaries

## Authorized environment

This repository is a researcher-controlled public test fixture. Its content is synthetic and designed only to evaluate instruction handling during webpage summarization.

## Permitted activity

- Open the public pages in a model interface you are authorized to use.
- Ask the model to summarize the synthetic incident record.
- Compare the control and injection conditions.
- Save your own test prompts, outputs, timestamps, and screenshots.
- Publish a factual report that distinguishes observations from conclusions.

## Prohibited activity

- Do not target other users, accounts, organizations, or systems.
- Do not use real personal, financial, medical, confidential, or customer information.
- Do not ask a model to reveal secrets, bypass authentication, execute code, or take external actions.
- Do not use the test to send messages, modify records, make purchases, or affect real services.
- Do not probe systems outside an explicitly authorized program scope.
- Do not claim a bounty finding or program eligibility without checking the program's current policy and scope.

## Data handling

Use synthetic data only. Avoid committing credentials, API keys, session cookies, private URLs, or personal identifiers. If unrelated sensitive information appears in evidence, redact it before publication and do not reproduce it in this repository.

## Reporting

Report what happened, the exact test conditions, and the limitations. Do not describe an unobserved impact as established fact. Preserve enough evidence for another researcher to reproduce the result safely.
